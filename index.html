PROJECT 01 · MAY – JULY 2025

Flight Mechanics and Thermoregulation in Daphnis nerii

Summer Research Project

  • Quantitatively analyzed wingbeat frequency and stroke amplitude during warm-up and sustained flight using high-speed videography.
  • Investigated the relationship between thoracic temperature dynamics and flight kinematics through computational analysis.
  • Utilized DLTdv and DeepLabCut for motion tracking and Python-based tools for data processing and visualization.

PROJECT 03 · JANUARY – MAY 2026

Biocontrol Allies: Strengthening Host Plants Against Salt Stress

Course Curriculum Reading Project

  • Conducted a literature-based investigation of plant growth-promoting bacteria (PGPB) and microbial biocontrol agents, including Bacillus, Pseudomonas and Geobacillus, in mitigating salinity-induced stress in plants.
  • Explored molecular and physiological mechanisms of salt-stress tolerance, focusing on Na+/K+ homeostasis, ROS scavenging, osmotic adjustment, antioxidant responses and phytohormone-mediated signalling.
  • Evaluated plant–microbe interactions and microbiome-mediated stress adaptation, including the effects of beneficial microbial inoculation on plant growth, disease resistance and host proteomic responses.
